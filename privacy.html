Privacy policy

Effective 17 October 2026

In short: Spotter collects no data. Everything you log stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the app makes no network connections.

What Spotter stores, and where

Your workouts, exercises, plans and settings are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. The developer can't see them and never receives them.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, iOS includes Spotter's data in that backup, as it does for most apps. Apple handles those backups under your own settings and Apple's privacy policy.

Copy and paste

Spotter reads the clipboard only when you tap Paste, to import a workout plan. It writes to the clipboard only when you tap a Copy button. If you paste text from Spotter into another app, such as an AI chat, that app's own privacy policy applies to what you share there.

Plan it for me

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Spotter can suggest today's session. It uses Apple's on-device model, which runs on your iPhone, so your history and anything you type for the plan never leave the phone. Spotter doesn't use Apple's cloud models. Apple Intelligence is part of iOS and covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Apple Health

If you turn on "Save workouts to Health" in Spotter's Profile, each session you finish is saved to Apple Health on your iPhone as a strength workout, with its start and end time. Spotter only writes to Health and reads nothing from it. Data in Health is never used for advertising and never sent to the developer. Other apps you allow to read Health can see these workouts under their own privacy policies. You can turn this off at any time in Spotter or in the Health app.

Notifications and the lock screen

The rest timer uses notifications and a Live Activity that your iPhone schedules and shows on its own. Nothing is sent to a server.

Deleting your data

You can delete any session in the app; if it was saved to Apple Health, its workout there is deleted too. Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

Spotter doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
