Spotter
The gym buddy who never flakes

The strength training app your phone should have shipped with.

Every lifter needs a spotter. Someone to count your rest, remember what you lifted last week, and hover their hands near the bar while you grind out the last rep. Spotter does the first two. The hovering is implied.

✅

Log like you mean it

Tap a set to check it off. Weight and reps are already filled in, and last time's numbers sit right next to them, quietly daring you to beat them.

⏱️

A rest timer that doesn't scroll Instagram

It starts the moment you check a set and lives on your lock screen, with −30 s, +30 s and Skip. No unlocking with chalky hands.

💬

Bring your own coach Beta

Copy Spotter's prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and ask for today's session. Paste it in, lift, and copy your log back. Or switch the AI part off and just lift.

🔒

Stays on your phone

No account, no ads, no tracking. Spotter collects no data at all. Your PR attempts are between you and the barbell.
