Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email spotter@mwahl.no. It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you tapped just before the problem.

How do I plan with an AI chat?

Open Settings in Spotter, make sure “Plan with an AI coach” is on, and tap “How to use Spotter with AI”. Copy the prompt into a new chat in the AI you use and answer its questions. Then ask it for today's session, copy the whole reply, and tap Paste on Spotter's Home screen. When you finish, tap “Copy log for AI” and paste the log back into the same chat.

Can I try it without an AI chat?

Yes. In the same guide, tap “Copy a sample plan”, then tap Paste on Home. Or skip AI altogether: tap Start session and pick your exercises.

The rest timer doesn't show on the lock screen

Check that Live Activities are allowed: iPhone Settings ▸ Spotter ▸ Live Activities.

I don't hear when rest is over

Check that notifications are allowed (iPhone Settings ▸ Notifications ▸ Spotter) and that a rest sound is chosen in Spotter's Settings. With the phone locked, the sound plays at your ringer volume and follows the silent switch.

Pounds instead of kilograms?

Spotter uses kilograms only for now.

How do I keep my data safe?

Everything is stored on your iPhone and included in your normal iPhone backups. Deleting the app deletes all of its data, so update it rather than reinstalling.
